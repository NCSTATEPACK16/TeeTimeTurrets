## 4. Facts pinned in M0 (don't rediscover them)

**Rapier 0.21** (`@dimforge/rapier3d-compat`, pinned):
- **Heightfield.** `heightfield(nrows, ncols, …)` takes subdivision counts. Rows run along +Z and columns along +X, column-major. Every cell is split along the (row, col+1)–(row+1, col) diagonal. Pinned by `test/physics/heightfieldLayout.test.ts`.
- **Edge rays.** Rays landing exactly on a grid line miss about a third of the time on bumpy terrain. Test points must stay off exact grid lines.
- **Vehicle direction and steering:**
  - Axle `(+1, 0, 0)` with suspension `(0, −1, 0)` makes positive engine force drive toward local −Z.
  - Positive steering turns **left**, so `RapierCart` negates the steer input.
  - `currentVehicleSpeed()` ignores `setIndexForwardAxis` (which is oddly a setter property, not a method). Use `RapierCart.forwardSpeed()`.
- **Brake.** `setWheelBrake` is an impulse cap per step (N·s), not a force, and Rapier only applies it to wheels whose engine force is 0.
- **Suspension.** Stiffness is per unit of chassis mass: static sag = g / (4 · stiffness).
- **Wheel spin.** `wheelRotation(i)` grows when the cart rolls toward −Z.
- **No body-roll control.** There is no `rollInfluence` in the JS API, so any anti-roll or yaw damping is your own force or impulse.
- **Signals for skid and spray.** Available per wheel: `wheelSideImpulse`, `wheelForwardImpulse`, `wheelSuspensionForce`, `wheelIsInContact`, `wheelContactPoint` and `wheelContactNormal`. There is no slip ratio; compute slip from the contact-point velocity (`linvel + angvel × r`) projected on the wheel's forward and axle directions.

**Chill baseline** (gate evidence, `dummy-01`):
- v(20 s) = 6.575 m/s, with no overshoot.
- 0→6 m/s in 3.32 s.
- Max roll 0.00° on the straight and 2.66° at full lock.
- Mean physics step 0.07–0.15 ms (budget: 2 ms per frame).
- `frictionSlip` 1.2. The center of mass sits 0.45 m below the chassis origin.

**three r186:**
- Import from `three/webgpu` and `three/tsl`. Post-processing goes through `RenderPipeline`; `PostProcessing` is deprecated.
- `scene.fogNode` and `scene.backgroundNode` are typed via module augmentation.
- `renderer.info.autoReset = false`, reset once per frame, so draw counts cover the whole pipeline.
- **WebGPU in Chromium 141.** three r186 puts `swizzle: 'rgba'` in texture-view descriptors. Chromium 141 rejects that string, then loses the device. The smoke test therefore runs only three's WebGL2 fallback, without `--enable-unsafe-webgpu`.

**Tooling:**
- **TypeScript 6.0.3**, not 7: typescript-eslint 8.71 requires TS < 6.1.
- **Vitest 5** hides console output from passing tests, so `vitest.config.ts` sets `silent: false`. Gate tests print their measurements with `console.log`.
- `.npmrc` sets `engine-strict` and `save-exact`.

**Packet:**
- Grids are W×H node samples. World x = col − (W−1)/2 and z = row − (H−1)/2. Row 0 is north (−Z).
- Heading is in compass degrees: 0 = −Z, 90 = +X.
- `course.json.qa` carries `driveStart`, an optional `wheelPad` and `probes`. M1 tests should stay packet-agnostic: read poses from `qa`, never hard-code coordinates, so M2 can rerun them on the real course with `GCS_LEVEL=<id>`. New qa fields are optional schema additions.

**Pipeline determinism.** Use only integer hashing and + − × ÷ √, with no numpy RNG, sin, exp, scipy or timestamps. JSON is written with sorted keys. This keeps the output byte-identical between x86 (cloud) and Apple Silicon (John).

## 6. M1 traps found so far (start the plan's challenge list here)

1. **Joyride top-speed test vs track length.** From `qa.driveStart` the straight runs 261 m (x = −135 to ≈126). Twenty seconds at ~13 m/s is about 260 m, so the cart would reach the curve. Either measure over ≤12 s, or lengthen the straight in `dummy.py` or add a dedicated `qa` run.
2. **Handbrake test needs Joyride and a steering rule.** Chill tops out at 6.7 m/s, so "handbrake at 10 m/s" implies Joyride, or setting the initial velocity directly in the test. The PRD doesn't say whether steering is applied, and a straight handbrake stop gives no yaw. Proposal: steady 10 m/s, then handbrake plus full steer, and assert peak yaw rate > 60°/s within 1.5 s. Agree the rule with John in the plan.
3. **"0.5 m water" doesn't exist as a flat area.** Pond depth runs 0–1.95 m. Only a sloped ring of about 220 cells is 0.4–0.6 m deep. Proposal: add a flat-bottomed 0.5 m wading pool to `dummy.py` plus an optional `qa.waterEntry` pose, then regenerate and commit, with the determinism tests still green. The entry speed of 10 m/s again needs Joyride or a set velocity.
4. **Water behavior is a new force model.** Drag ∝ speed² × depth below the water line. Beyond 0.6 m the motor stalls and the cart **floats**, which means a buoyancy force. Chill respawns the cart on the nearest path after 3 s, which needs a nearest-point lookup over `paths.json`; Joyride bobs until reset. None of this exists yet.
5. **Skid ribbons conflict with "persist for the session."** The PRD wants Joyride marks to persist all session, but caps the ribbon at 4 wheels × 512 segments in a ring buffer, which overwrites the oldest marks. Propose "persist until the buffer wraps" or a larger Joyride buffer. The ribbon also sits 1 cm above the ground, which will z-fight on slopes, so plan a depth bias.
6. **Skid emitter test.** Make the emitter a pure function of per-wheel state (on ground, speed > 2 m/s, side slip or brake lock over a threshold, all thresholds in config). Then gate item 3 is a plain unit test with cases just below and just above each threshold.
7. **Rollover rules are an open question.** The modes table says Joyride "flips allowed and funny" with assist only after 2 s on its side, and Chill assists above 25° roll. PRD open question 2 asks whether the cart can flip at all. Ask John. Also make sure the Chill assist doesn't hide regressions: keep a raw-chassis roll test with the assist off.
8. **Boost has no definition.** "Boost to ~15 m/s" has no input, duration or recharge. PRD open question 1 (is 30 mph the right cap?) is also unanswered.
9. **Trees.** In M0, trees are not colliders, which was flagged as an M1 decision for John. The surface model's "never stuck" rule and stuck recovery both interact with this.
10. **Pine straw** still has no material ID. Woods ground stands in for it, so give woods the "Rough, pine straw" surface row.
