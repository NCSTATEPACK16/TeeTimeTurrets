**Packet v0.2** (`packages/level-schema/schema/` is the single source of truth):
- Grids are W×H node samples with 1 m spacing, centered on the origin. Row 0 is north (−Z).
- Height = offset + scale × u16, so the default scale of 0.001 m allows 65.5 m of relief. Check Caswell's relief and raise the scale if needed.
- Materials are IDs 0–9, append-only.
- `water[]` entries are `{id, level, polygon}`. `scatter.bin` holds float32 records of x, z, scale and rotation.
- `crs` is `{epsg, originEasting, originNorthing}` for node (0, 0).
- `course.json.qa` holds `driveStart`, optional `wheelPad`, optional `waterEntry` and `probes`.
- `horizon.u16.bin` is **not in the schema yet**. Add it as an optional field.

**Web (`apps/web/src/`):** the samplers, colliders, cart, modes, effects and audio all read only the packet.
- `WaterSampler` handles several bodies.
- `PathSampler` gives the nearest point on the cart paths.
- Trees in `scatter.bin` get trunk colliders (`level/treeConfig.ts`).
- Terrain renders as 128-cell chunks with no LOD. A real course is larger than the dummy's 513², so watch the triangle and draw-call caps: ≤2 M triangles and ≤300 draw calls on High.

**Locked conventions:** `CLAUDE.md` plus `docs/handoff/M1.md` section 4 (Rapier facts, three r186, tooling). Pipeline determinism means integer hashing and correctly rounded arithmetic only, with no RNG streams, sin, exp, scipy filters or timestamps in the **output**. Real-data steps (Gaussian smoothing, reprojection) can't follow that rule exactly. Decide what "byte-deterministic" means for the real packet: same machine and same input give the same bytes, and the committed packet is the reference.

### Traps (start the plan's challenge list here)
1. **Network.** USGS 3DEP, the TNM API, NC's lidar portal and Overpass are all blocked in the cloud. Per `CLAUDE.md`, stop and name the domains. Either John allows them (Part A, option a), or the scripts run on his Mac.
   - Don't fabricate terrain to fill the gap.
   - Unit-test every pipeline step on small synthetic fixtures so the cloud can still prove the code.
2. **Which lidar.** NC has statewide QL2 lidar, whose tiles are often in NC State Plane US survey feet (EPSG:6543 or 2264); the PRD's "convert US survey feet" refers to this. USGS 3DEP 1 m DEMs are in meters, in UTM or Albers. Confirm what covers Caswell County and its vertical datum before writing `dem.py`.
3. **OSM coverage is unknown.** Caswell Pines may have no `golf=*` polygons or `golf=cartpath` lines.
   - The fallbacks are hand-drawn `overrides.geojson` (traced from aerial imagery John supplies, never from the flyover stills) and A* paths.
   - Check coverage first. It decides how much of M2 is authoring.
4. **"All M0/M1 tests pass on the real packet" conflicts with how optional pads work.** Several tests skip when the packet has no `qa.wheelPad` or `qa.waterEntry`.
   - Real ponds have no flat 0.5 m wading area, and the course has no 4-material pad.
   - `forward.test.ts` hard-codes a slope point at (0, −175) that only makes sense on `dummy-01`.
   - `joyrideTopSpeed` needs a straight, nearly flat run of about 160 m from `qa.driveStart` (12 s); a real cart path may not have one.
   - Agree with John whether a skip counts as a pass, or have the pipeline author qa poses (a long fairway run, a probe per material) and keep dummy-only tests on `dummy-01`.
5. **Grid size and performance.** Eighteen holes cover very roughly 1.5 × 1.2 km.
   - That is about 1.8 M nodes and about 3.6 M terrain triangles: over the 2 M budget, and over 100 chunks.
   - Rapier's heightfield and the packet size (the PRD caps the first download at 60 MB) are fine at that size.
   - Rendering needs LOD or bigger, coarser far chunks. The hole-1-only packet avoids this at first.
6. **Hole 1 first means a partial packet.** Define its extent (hole 1 plus a margin and the cart-path link to the clubhouse) and how it grows to 18 without breaking IDs or tests.
7. **Sculpt report location.** It goes in `pipeline/reports/<id>/` (gitignored), not under `public/`, which Vite ships. `.gitignore` already covers it.
8. **Attribution.** OSM data is ODbL, so add the notice to `ATTRIBUTION.md` and to `course.json.attribution`. USGS data is public domain. NC lidar terms need checking.
9. **Name in builds.** Until John answers Part A step 3, give the packet a neutral `id` and `name` (for example `id: "course-01"`).
