# Back 9 reference notes (DRAFT)

Source: drone flyover video, "Back 9", 720p, about 4:33. Stills in `stills/`, named
`holeNN_<k>_t<seconds>.jpg` (6 per hole, evenly spaced tee to green). `sheets/` has one
3x2 sheet per hole plus four overview sheets (one frame every 5 s).

**Private reference only.** The footage is third-party and carries the club's logo
and an on-screen graphic. Keep `data/reference/` in `.gitignore`. Do not commit these
stills, ship them in builds, or trace them into game assets.

**How these notes were made.** From still frames only. Par, handicap and yardages are
read from the on-screen graphic (cropped at full resolution). Everything under "Seen in
footage" is what is visible in the stills. Directions (left/right) are as seen from the
tee looking toward the green. Nothing here is measured; use lidar and OSM for geometry.

## Seen in footage

Video time windows are approximate. Black-frame cuts mark most hole changes.

### Hole 10, par 3, handicap 5 (about 0:00 to 0:19)
- Tee box with a green marker, pines left and a mix of pines and hardwoods right; cart path on the left.
- Open, gently rolling approach with a sand/waste patch short right of the green.
- Green sits in front of a dense tree line, with mounds around it and a cart path along the left.

### Hole 11, par 4, handicap 15 (about 0:22 to 0:38)
- Tee is elevated behind a dense shrub and hardwood screen. A cart path curves down the left of the fairway.
- Wide striped fairway. Pines with bright red pine-straw beds on the right in the landing area; one lone hardwood left of center.
- Fairway runs down to a small raised green with a pale bunker sweeping across the front. Looks like a short, easy hole.
- Cart path crosses the fairway before the green and continues along the right.

### Hole 12, par 4, handicap 3 (about 0:40 to 1:07)
- Long mown tee with a tall pine stand on the right; cart path runs along the right side.
- Wide, striped fairway that rolls and crests, with a tree line behind.
- A creek or drainage cut with a small wooden bridge crosses in front of the green, and the cart path crosses with it.
- Green is an oval with a dark collar, sitting low in a wooded hollow with a steep bank behind.

### Hole 13, par 4, handicap 11 (about 1:12 to 1:42)
- Elevated tee over a long pond that runs down the left side of the hole; reeds and shrubs along the edge.
- A cart path hugs the water on the right. Tall pines with red straw line the far side.
- Three small bunkers sit on the far side of the pond by the fairway. A larger bunker sits short of the green, next to the water.
- Green is peanut-shaped with a collar, pond close on the left.

### Hole 14, par 5, handicap 13 (about 1:43 to 2:12)
- Green on 13 is visible in the first frame, with the cart path leaving it toward the next tee.
- Long, wide fairway between tall pines on the right and hardwoods on the left; cart path on the right.
- A small separate patch of mown grass (probably a forward tee) sits in the fairway.
- Fairway undulates, with mounds near the green.
- Green is small and wide, flanked by big bunkers on both sides (pale sand). Trees behind.

### Hole 15, par 4, handicap 9 (about 2:17 to 2:47)
- Tee sits on the left on a rise; cart path curves down the right. A trailer or small building shows on the right.
- Wide, open fairway with a bunker on the right in the landing area. Hardwoods left and a big tree right near the green.
- Pines with red straw close in toward the green; a small pond is visible beyond and right of the green.
- Green is striped, with a large pink-salmon bunker front right. Cart path wraps around the right.

### Hole 16, par 4, handicap 1 (about 2:48 to 3:17)
- Tee has a cart path that sweeps across the front. Hardwoods and pines frame the opening.
- Wide, striped fairway with a lone hardwood right of center. A cart path winds down the right.
- Fairway runs broad and open toward a pine stand. A house with a pale roof is visible behind the green.
- Green sits slightly raised with a cart path coming in from the right. Pine straw beds and shrubs behind.

### Hole 17, par 3, handicap 17 (about 3:19 to 3:37)
- Shaded tee in a pine corridor. Cart path on the right; pine straw and bare ground at the right edge.
- Approach is partly in shade and has a worn or sandy patch before the green.
- Green is wide and shallow, with a bunker on the right and one front left. Houses are visible through the trees behind.

### Hole 18, par 5, handicap 7 (about 3:38 to 4:12)
- The first frame at 3:41 still shows a flag and houses behind a green, so it is likely the end of hole 17 or a transition.
- Long, wide fairway, nearly straight. Pines on both sides with red straw; cart path on the left.
- Hardwood group right. A fairway bunker sits on the right further down.
- A pond comes into play on the right near the green, with reeds along the edge. A big pale bunker sits short right.
- Green is oval with a bunker behind it and a cart path along the left.

## Course-wide observations (useful for art and materials)
- Back nine looks more open than the front. The same loblolly pine and red straw look, but more water (ponds on 13, 15, 18) and a creek on 12.
- Cart paths are dark asphalt, narrow and curvy, and they cross fairways on 11, 12 and 15.
- Bunker sand is mostly pale tan; hole 15 reads pink-salmon.
- Greens are ovals, kidneys or peanuts with tight collars. Houses are visible behind 16, 17 and 18.
