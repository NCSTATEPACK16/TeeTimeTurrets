at his request on 2026-10-18 so every session has them. They are text written by us.

The flyover stills and contact sheets they mention are **not** committed: they are third-party

(gitignored), from `caswell_pines_front9_reference.zip` and `caswell_pines_back9_reference.zip`.
Use them to check layout and as art direction only; geometry comes from the lidar and the aerial.
