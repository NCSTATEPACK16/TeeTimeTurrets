Source: drone flyover video, "Front 9", 720p, 289 s. Stills in `stills/`, named
`holeNN_<k>_t<seconds>.jpg` (6 per hole, evenly spaced tee to green).

**Private reference only.** The footage is third-party and carries the club's logo
and an on-screen graphic. Keep `data/reference/` in `.gitignore`. Do not commit these
stills, ship them in builds, or trace them into game assets.

read from the on-screen graphic (checked at full resolution). Everything under "Seen in
footage" is what is visible in the stills. Directions (left/right) are as seen from the
tee looking toward the green. Nothing here is measured; use lidar and OSM for geometry.

## Seen in footage

Video time windows are approximate. Black-frame cuts mark most hole changes; holes 2 to 3
and 6 to 7 change without a cut.

### Hole 1, par 5 (about 0:20 to 0:52)
- Cart path starts at the tee on the left, runs up the fairway, and a branch crosses the fairway partway down.
- Tall pine stand with red pine-straw beds on the left; hardwoods and a low shrub line on the right.
- Wide fairway, one lone tree right of center in the landing area, fairway bunkers on the right.
- Green is small with two bunkers on the front right; a cart path passes behind and left of it.
- Looks fairly straight. Open sky and a tall radio tower in the distance.

### Hole 2, par 4 (about 0:54 to 1:23)
- Tee sits left with hardwoods close on the right; cart path winds down the right side, with rope and stakes along it.
- Wide, rolling fairway with a building (red roof) visible left near the start.
- Pines line the left near the green; fairway bunker on the right in the landing area.
- Large oval green, with a big sweeping bunker on the right side. The sand has an orange-red cast.

### Hole 3, par 4, handicap 2 (about 1:29 to 1:57)
- Long tee box, pines tight on the left, dense hardwoods on the right; cart path runs down the right.
- Fairway narrows and rolls toward a green set low in a wooded hollow.
- Short rock or stone edging across the front of the green area, a small pond to the right of the approach, and a bunker right of the green.
- Cart path wraps around the right side of the green.

### Hole 4, par 3 (about 2:00 to 2:17)
- Elevated tee (a round green tee marker in the first frame) looking over two ponds: one mid-distance left, one large in the foreground right.
- Green sits beyond and left of the water with a bunker front right; pines behind, houses visible through the trees.
- Cart path runs along the right of the water.

### Hole 5, par 4, handicap 18 (about 2:20 to 2:47)
- Long narrow tee box, pines on both sides with deep red pine-straw beds; cart path with reddish surface on the left.
- Fairway is wide and rolling, with mounding in front of the green.
- Bunker front left of the green with pink-salmon sand, clearly different from the tan sand elsewhere.
- Green sits against a tree line.

### Hole 6, par 3 (about 2:52 to 3:08)
- Large tee complex with two tee pads; cart path curves along the left.
- Open, undulating ground between tee and green with several swales and low mounds.
- Green is peanut-shaped, with two bunkers on its left side and mounds around it. Trees and houses far behind.

### Hole 7, par 4 (about 3:10 to 3:43)
- Narrow cart path winds up from the tee on the left.
- Fairway climbs gently between pines and hardwoods; large bunker left in the landing area.
- Green is peanut-shaped with a bunker in front and one behind. Cart path wraps right.

### Hole 8, par 5 (about 3:44 to 4:12)
- Cart path snakes from the tee across and down the left side.
- Long, nearly straight fairway between pines on the left and hardwoods on the right; a house with a brown roof and a fence show on the right.
- Fairway bunker right of the landing area.
- Kidney-shaped green with two large bunkers short of it (bigger one on the left, smaller on the right).

### Hole 9, par 4, handicap 4 (about 4:15 to 4:47)
- Path curves down the left of the fairway; pines left, mixed hardwoods right.
- Fairway crests and then drops to a green set in a tree-ringed hollow.
- Log cabin left of the green is a clear landmark. Bunker right of the green.
- A path leads from the green to the next tee area, where a small sign is visible.

## Course-wide observations (useful for art and materials)
- Loblolly pines with red clay and pine-straw beds are the signature look. Hardwoods fill the rest.
- Cart paths are dark asphalt, narrow and curvy. They are not drawn as straight lines.
- Bunker sand is mostly pale tan. Hole 2 reads orange-red and hole 5 reads pink.
- Greens are smooth ovals, kidneys or peanuts with tight collars. The ground around them is mounded.
- Fairways are mowed with visible stripes and run wide in the landing areas.
