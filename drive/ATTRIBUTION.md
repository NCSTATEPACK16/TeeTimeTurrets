## Data

The M0 level `dummy-01` is fully synthetic (generated by `pipeline`'s `gce-dummy`). The M2 course
`caswell-pines` is built from the public data below; its layout is hand-authored over the aerial
(`pipeline/courses/caswell-pines/overrides.geojson`).

### NC 2024 QL1 lidar DEMs

Course terrain (3 ft DEM) and the horizon skirt (20 ft DEM) come from the North Carolina 2024 QL1
lidar county DEM mosaics, NC Emergency Management / NC Spatial Data Download
(<https://sdd.nc.gov/>). NC public data; the exact terms of use are **still to confirm**.

### NC OneMap orthoimagery

`pipeline/courses/caswell-pines/aerial/ortho.tif` (NC Center for Geographic Information and
Analysis, NC 911 Board, via NC OneMap) is the tracing base for the authored layout and the source of
the tree-canopy mask. It is not shipped in any packet. NC public data; terms **still to confirm**.

### OpenStreetMap

`pipeline/courses/caswell-pines/osm/` holds a snapshot of OpenStreetMap data (golf features, water,
paths, woods, buildings) used to lay out the Caswell Pines course.
© OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the
[Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).
See <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>.

## Assets

### Caswell Pines Golf Club name and logo

`pipeline/courses/caswell-pines/logo.webp` (copied into the `caswell-pines` packet) is the club's logo, and the course appears under its
real name. Both are used with the club's permission, as relayed by John Bradner (2026-10-17).
Whether that permission is in writing is **still to confirm**.

## Fonts

The menus and HUD use **Shrikhand** (Jonny Pinhorn) and **Rubik** (Hubert and Fischer, Meir
Sadan, Cyreal), bundled from the `@fontsource/shrikhand` and `@fontsource/rubik` packages and
served with the game (never fetched from a font host). Both are under the
[SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/).

## Artwork

The title screen, ride select and HUD art (`apps/web/src/ui/art.ts`, `apps/web/src/ui/theme.css`)
is drawn for this project in SVG and CSS. The turret cart follows the cart concept from the
TeeTimeTurrets project (same owner); no concept image is shipped or traced.
